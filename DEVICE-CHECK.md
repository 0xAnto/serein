# Device check

1. Install the APK. Open Serein and play Sleep. Confirm its Android media controls appear.
2. Switch apps, lock the phone for five minutes, and confirm playback continues. Use lock-screen pause and resume.
3. Set a one-minute custom timer with a 15-second fade. Confirm the player countdown, notification, fade, and final pause. Pause and resume halfway to verify the countdown pauses too.
4. Open Mixes from the bottom navigation, select multiple sounds, then tap the player name to change two layer volumes. Save a mix and replay it from Home after restarting Serein. Long-press its card to edit or delete it.
5. Add both widgets. Test play/pause, all preset buttons, and timer cycling without opening the app.
6. Use the top-right options menu to toggle light and night palettes. Check Home, Mixes, Breathe, and every dialog. Increase the device font size and check the player controls, card labels, and timer. Check portrait, landscape, gesture navigation, and three-button navigation for clipped edges.
7. Disconnect from the network. Every sound and widget should still work.
8. Use a second audio app or a phone call. Confirm sensible ducking or pause, and unplug headphones to confirm pause.
